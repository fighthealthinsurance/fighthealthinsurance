What we try to keep in your browser

We try to keep your name and street address in your browser, like a cookie, rather than on our server. You can clear them in your browser or from our privacy policy page. If you join the mailing list, we send your name and email to our server for it.

How we store your email

We aim to store only a scrambled version of your email (a hash), so you can ask us to remove it without us keeping your real address. If you turn on follow-ups or faxing, we keep your real email so we can remind you when the insurer's deadlines may have passed, and we may email you a better draft if our first one wasn't good. You can use a temporary email service, like Proton Mail. If you join the mailing list, we keep your name and real email for it. Our privacy policy has the details.
