The address

Both assistants connect to the same address. Copy it as it is, with no sign-in or password:

https://www.fighthealthinsurance.com/mcp

The connection uses MCP, the open standard assistants use to talk to tools, so other assistants and coding tools that speak it work the same way.

Set it up in Claude

Works on every Claude plan, including the free one, on the web, the desktop app and the phone apps.

Add to Claude

That link opens Claude with the form already filled in. To do it by hand:

  1. Open Customize, then Connectors, press + Add and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Fight Health Insurance, paste the address above as the MCP server URL and press Continue. There is nothing to sign in to.
  3. Open the connector's page and check Tool permissions. It should list the tools described below.
  4. In a chat, open the + menu, choose Connectors and turn Fight Health Insurance on. Then ask your question. Claude asks before each tool it uses; Always allow stops the asking for that tool.

Set it up in ChatGPT

ChatGPT needs a paid plan (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Education) and its developer mode, which is a switch in settings.

  1. Open Settings, then Security and login, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Go to Plugins, press Add, and choose Create MCP App. {% comment %}figure slot: chatgpt-1-plugins-add{% endcomment %}
  3. Name it Fight Health Insurance, paste the address above, choose No authentication, and create it. {% comment %}figure slot: chatgpt-2-create-app{% endcomment %}
  4. In a chat, open the + menu, choose Developer mode and pick the app. Then ask your question. ChatGPT asks before it uses a tool that changes anything; the look-up tools run on their own. {% comment %}figure slot: chatgpt-3-answer{% endcomment %}

What it can do

Try: "I got a denial for an MRI, where do I start?" The assistant looks up what to gather for an appeal, the guide for MRI denials and the link to start one here, and answers with those links. Everything it can look up is public information from this site:

Your rights and deadlines

Which appeal law covers your kind of plan, the usual deadlines, and what to have ready before you start.

Your state

Who regulates health insurance where you live and who gives free help there.

Your insurer

Where a health insurer takes appeals: portal, fax, mailing address, email and phone, with the insurer's own page as the source.

The denial's wording

What a reason like "not medically necessary" means, why insurers use it and how people answer it.

Treatment guides and money

Our guide to appealing a denial of a specific treatment, drug or test, and copay foundations, drug-maker programs and safety-net programs that can help pay.

This site's pages

A search of the guides, FAQs, state pages, blog posts and glossary, and any page read as plain text. Every answer links back here.

It is general information, not legal or medical advice.

What to keep out of the chat

A short treatment or condition word, such as "MRI" or "migraine", is all the assistant needs to find a guide or financial help. Keep everything else personal out of these tools: your denial letter, your name, member ID and medical history belong on this site, where you can remove personal details before anything is sent anywhere. The assistant is told the same thing when it connects.

What your assistant itself keeps from a chat is up to its maker; see Anthropic's privacy policy or OpenAI's. What we keep is in our privacy policy.
