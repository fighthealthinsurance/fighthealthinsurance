  • Where people get coverage: FHI's tally of the U.S. Census Bureau's Current Population Survey, 2026 Annual Social and Economic Supplement, from its public-use file (asecpub26csv.zip, released September 15, 2026), for people of all ages as of their interviews from February to April 2026. Each person is counted once: anyone with Medicare under Medicare, anyone else with exactly one kind of coverage under that kind, and anyone else with two or more kinds under "more than one kind". Our tally matches the totals and margins of error Census publishes from the same survey (report P60-291, Table 1, and table H-02). Every share is within half a point either way, at 90% confidence. Medicare includes Medicare Advantage plans, which private insurers run. Job-based includes self-funded plans, where the employer pays the claims itself. Medicaid or CHIP includes other state programs for people with low incomes and Basic Health Programs, such as New York's Essential Plan. VA includes CHAMPVA, and someone with both TRICARE and VA counts under "more than one kind".
  • Why this survey: it gives marketplace plans their own category and lets us count each person once. Census's American Community Survey has no marketplace category, and Census changed its coverage question for 2025, so it warns against comparing 2025 with earlier years. Census's own published shares from this survey add up to more than 100%, since people can have more than one kind. The CDC's National Health Interview Survey is a second survey, but its latest figures are for 2025 and its marketplace figures are for people under 65, so it can't show everyone in early 2026.
  • Surveys and records: CMS's records count 19.2 million people with marketplace coverage in February 2026, 68.9 million on Medicare and 75.5 million on Medicaid or CHIP. Only surveys count job-based coverage and the uninsured. Census's survey finds fewer people with marketplace plans than CMS's records count. CMS counts plans in effect in February, the survey asks from February to April, and some coverage ends in between.
  • Medicare and Medicaid: Medicare is CMS's Medicare Monthly Enrollment count for February 2026, from the file with data through July 2026, the 50 states and DC added up; it includes Medicare Advantage. Medicaid and CHIP is February 2026's updated count for the 50 states and DC, from CMS's State Medicaid and CHIP Applications, Eligibility Determinations, and Enrollment Data (the September 2026 release). People with both Medicare and Medicaid are in both counts. Checked October 26, 2026.
  • What Denial Watch's rates cover: {% if here == "dw" %}This page's{% else %}Denial Watch's{% endif %} claim denial rates are for marketplace plans in HealthCare.gov states and five states that run their own marketplace, where some figures also count plans sold outside it. Its prior authorization rates cover large Medicare Advantage plans, Medicaid plans and programs, and HealthCare.gov insurers. It has no denial rates for job-based plans or Original Medicare.
