CMS's records count 19.2 million people with marketplace coverage in February 2026, 68.9 million on Medicare and 75.5 million on Medicaid or CHIP (some people have both Medicare and Medicaid). Only surveys count job-based coverage and the uninsured, and surveys find fewer marketplace plans than records show.
