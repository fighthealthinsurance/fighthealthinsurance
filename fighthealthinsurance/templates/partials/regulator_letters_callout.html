Alongside your appeal to the insurer, you can write to the people who hold the plan accountable. We draft those letters for you. Here is how it works:

  1. Click the button below. The letters open in a new tab, and this page stays where it is.
  2. We draft one letter per recipient: your state Department of Insurance (when you told us your state), the plan's medical director, and, for ERISA self-funded plans, the U.S. Department of Labor's EBSA. Each takes about 1 to 2 minutes.
  3. Edit each letter, save it, then print or fax it alongside your appeal.

You don't have to wait for your appeal drafts or pick one first. The letters are written from your denial.
