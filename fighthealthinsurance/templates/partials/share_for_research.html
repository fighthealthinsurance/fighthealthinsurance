What does sharing mean?

Ticking the box saves a separate copy of these documents for research, with what you've told us about the plan. It changes nothing else here. If you leave the box unticked, we make no copy.

We keep the files encrypted. Our team and AI models, including outside services, read them with documents from many other people, and we share what we learn publicly. If you ask us to delete your data, we delete your files, including this copy. What we've learned about the plan, like what kind of plan it is, stays, because it describes the plan, not you.
